Delete Your YT ASSISTANT Account

This page explains how users of the YT ASSISTANT Android app can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to the developer using the contact email shown on the YT ASSISTANT Google Play Store listing.
  2. Use the subject line: YT ASSISTANT Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address used to sign in to YT ASSISTANT so the account can be identified.
  4. Request deletion of your account and associated data.

What data may be deleted?

Account information associated with the app will be reviewed for deletion. Any locally stored history can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Information that must be retained to comply with legal obligations may be kept for the required period.

Processing time

The developer will review the request and respond by email. If any data must be retained, the user will be informed where applicable.

Contact

Use the developer contact email listed on the YT ASSISTANT Google Play Store listing to submit an account deletion request.

Privacy Policy for YT ASSISTANT

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

YT ASSISTANT helps users create YouTube content using AI-powered tools. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use the app.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may process information you provide, such as prompts, topics, and text submitted for AI generation. If you sign in with Google, authentication information made available by Google may be processed to manage your account.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide AI-generated content, authenticate users, maintain app functionality, manage subscriptions, remember your history and preferences, and display advertisements.

4. AI Services

Prompts submitted for AI generation may be sent to the AI service provider to produce results. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive personal information in prompts.

5. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google authentication services are used to authenticate your account. Google's own privacy policy also applies to information handled by Google.

6. Advertising

The app may display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements. Advertising providers may process device identifiers and usage information as described in their own privacy policies and settings.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

The app may offer paid subscription features through Google Play Billing. Payment transactions are handled by Google Play. The app does not directly collect or store your complete payment card details.

8. Local History and Preferences

Some app history and preferences may be stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored information.

9. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate app features, including authentication, AI generation, advertising, and subscription management. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

10. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information is retained only as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

11. Children's Privacy

YT ASSISTANT is not intended for children under 13, or the applicable minimum age in their country where higher. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. Your Choices

You can stop using the app at any time. You may also manage Google account permissions and advertising preferences through the relevant Google settings.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published at this URL.

14. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact details provided on the app's Google Play listing.
